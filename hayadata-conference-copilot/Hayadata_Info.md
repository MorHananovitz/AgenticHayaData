# hayaData 2026: conference information

Date: 20 October 2026. Venue: David InterContinental Hotel, 12 Kaufmann St., Tel Aviv. Contact: team@haya-data.com. All times are Israel time.

## Registration and entry

- Registration and entry begin at 08:30.
- Bring your ticket and a valid photo ID. Both are required at the registration desk.
- Entry is permitted only to the person whose name appears on the ticket. A ticket cannot be used by anyone other than the registered attendee.
- Tickets can be transferred only before the day of the conference.
- To transfer a ticket, email team@haya-data.com with the new attendee's full name, email address, phone number, job title, and company name. The hayaData team takes care of the transfer.

## Conference app

The organizers highly recommend the conference agenda app. With it you can highlight talks you are interested in, check live sessions, explore speaker details, and more. Download it at https://hayadata-2026.sessionize.com/

## Getting there

### Public transportation
The organizers encourage using public transportation whenever possible.

- Light rail: take the Red Line to Elifelet Station, about a 15-minute walk from the venue.
- Bus: several lines serve stops within a 2 to 5 minute walk of the venue, including 10, 16, 17, 18, 31, 54, and 168. Nearby stops include Carmelit Terminal, Charles Clore Park/Kaufmann St., and Yosef Levi/HaKovshim.
- For routes and up-to-date schedules, check Moovit (https://moovitapp.com/) or Google Maps.

### Parking
- Parking is available in the hotel parking lot at a discounted rate of 70 NIS.
- Validate your parking ticket at the discount parking machine on the conference floor.

## Food and drinks

- The conference provides a light breakfast, lunch, and coffee breaks, with hot and cold drinks available throughout the day.
- Vegan, vegetarian, and gluten-free options are offered, along with milk alternatives such as soy and oat.
- The food preparation and serving areas are not entirely gluten-free. Cross-contamination with gluten and other allergens may occur.
- All food is kosher. Kosher certificate: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Dst8QHLUiGTHS38Y_STequKv0vYvx5eT/view?usp=sharing
- Glatt-kosher meals are available on request at the start of the day.
- For any questions, consult the hayaData team.

## Nursing room

Babies and strollers are welcome at hayaData. There is a dedicated nursing room near the elevators. It can be locked from the inside for privacy and comfort. Any hayaData team member can guide you to it.

## Emergency

If an alarm sounds during the conference, remain where you are. The conference space is designated as a protected and safe area. The event is conducted in full compliance with Home Front Command guidelines.

## Code of conduct

The conference is dedicated to a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of gender, gender identity and expression, age, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, or religion.

- Harassment includes offensive verbal comments, sexual images in public spaces, deliberate intimidation, stalking, harassing photography or recording, sustained disruption of talks or other events, inappropriate physical contact, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Participants asked to stop harassing behavior are expected to comply immediately.
- Harassment of participants is not tolerated in any form. Sexual language and imagery are inappropriate at any conference venue, including talks, workshops, parties, and online media.
- Violators may be sanctioned or expelled from the conference without a refund, at the organizers' discretion.
- Sponsors are also subject to the anti-harassment policy. Sponsors should not use sexualized images, activities, or other material. Booth staff, including volunteers, should not use sexualized clothing, uniforms, or costumes or otherwise create a sexualized environment.
- If you are being harassed, notice someone else being harassed, or have any other concerns, contact a member of conference staff immediately. Staff wear branded clothing and/or badges. They can help participants contact venue security or local law enforcement, provide escorts, or otherwise help anyone experiencing harassment feel safe.
- The same rules apply at conference-related social events.
- Full text: https://www.haya-data.com/code-of-conduct/
